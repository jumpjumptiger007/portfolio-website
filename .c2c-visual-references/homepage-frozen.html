INTER DIMENSIONAL CABLE

A retro-futurist web television for random signal hunting and internet wandering. A generated catalog becomes one shuffled receiver sequence: tune forward or backward, change the signal, save a favorite, or permanently skip a channel.

TECHNICAL DETAIL

The browser reads a generated catalog rather than fetching Reddit at runtime. Playback remains hosted by YouTube, while favorites, permanent skips, saved channel, volume, mute state, random mode and ambience remain local to the browser.
